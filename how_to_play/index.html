How to Play

2D Grand Prix is a top-down 2D racing video game. Your objective in a Grand Prix is to score the most points of any racer, and finish in first place overall.

Placing higher gives you more points:

  • 1st: 5 points
  • 2nd: 3 points
  • 3rd: 1 point
  • 4th: 0 points

The game controls are as follows. A keyboard is required:

  • w: drive forward
  • s: drive backward
  • a: turn left
  • d: turn right
  • 1 (debug): activate debug mode
  • 2 (debug): deactivate debug mode
  • 3 (debug): end race immediately (use current positions of racers as their final positions and load the results screen)
